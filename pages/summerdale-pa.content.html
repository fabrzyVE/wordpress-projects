Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Arkview from Summerdale?
Our Mechanicsburg campus is about 7 miles from Summerdale — roughly a 12 minute drive.
Is Arkview currently accepting new admissions?
Yes — we admit 24/7, and most admissions happen within 24–48 hours of your first call.
Does Arkview accept my insurance in Pennsylvania?
We're in-network with most major commercial plans. Call and we'll verify your benefits at no cost, usually within minutes.
What's the difference between residential and outpatient treatment?
Residential means living on-site with around-the-clock care. Outpatient (PHP and IOP) means structured treatment during the day while you live at home in Summerdale.
Does Arkview treat mental health conditions alongside addiction?
Yes — separate, dedicated programs for each, so both get focused care.
How long does treatment typically last?
A few days for detox, several weeks for residential and outpatient.
